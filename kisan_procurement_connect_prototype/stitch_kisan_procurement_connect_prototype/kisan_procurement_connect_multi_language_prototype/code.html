Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a procurement slot? expand_more

Go to Dashboard, tap 'Book Slot', choose your nearest APMC mandi yard, pick date and time, and confirm.

How does the digital queue work? expand_more

Once you arrive at the gate, your token is activated. Your live queue updates automatically showing how many tractors are ahead.

How to check payment and MSP status? expand_more

Tap 'Procurement Status' or 'My Bookings' to view the weighing slip, moisture check result, and DBT bank account deposit status.
