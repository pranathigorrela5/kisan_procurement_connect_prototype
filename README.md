# Kisan Procurement Connect

## About the Project

Kisan Procurement Connect is a web-based platform created to make the crop procurement process easier for farmers. Farmers often have to wait for long hours at procurement centres and may not have clear information about schedules, queues, or the status of their crops.

This platform helps farmers get the required information in one place. They can check procurement schedules, book a slot, track their queue position, and see the current status of their procurement.

## Problem Statement

Farmers may face several problems during the procurement process, such as:

* Long waiting times at procurement centres
* Not knowing the correct procurement date and time
* Difficulty in getting a procurement slot
* No clear information about their queue position
* Lack of updates about the procurement process
* Difficulty in knowing when the payment will be processed

## Our Solution

Kisan Procurement Connect provides a simple platform where farmers can manage their procurement activities without having to depend completely on manual enquiries.

Farmers can:

* Check procurement schedules
* Book a suitable slot
* Track their live queue
* Check procurement status
* Receive important notifications
* View previous and upcoming bookings
* Check payment-related updates
* Contact support when they need help

## Key Features

### Farmer Registration and Login

Farmers can create an account using their basic details and log in to access the platform.

### Procurement Schedule

Farmers can check the available dates, time slots, and procurement centre details before visiting.

### Slot Booking

Farmers can choose an available slot and book it according to their convenience.

### Live Queue Tracking

Farmers can see their current queue number and get an idea of how many people are ahead of them.

### Procurement Status

The farmer can follow the progress of their crop through different stages:

Booked → Arrived → Quality Check → Weighing → Procurement Completed → Payment

### Notifications

The system provides updates about bookings, schedules, queue changes, and procurement status.

### My Bookings

Farmers can view their current bookings as well as their previous bookings.

### Multiple Languages

The platform is designed to support English, Telugu, and Hindi so that it can be easier for farmers from different regions to use.

### Help and Support

Farmers can find helpline and support information whenever they need assistance.

## Benefits

The platform can help:

* Reduce unnecessary waiting time
* Give farmers better information about procurement schedules
* Make the procurement process more organized
* Reduce confusion about queue positions
* Provide clear updates about procurement status
* Improve communication between farmers and procurement centres

## How the System Works

The basic process is:

Register/Login → View Schedule → Select Procurement Centre → Book Slot → Get Confirmation → Track Queue → Visit Centre → Quality Check → Weighing → Procurement Completed → Payment Update
